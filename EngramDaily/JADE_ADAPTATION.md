# Jade: canon and companion adaptation

Source: Beck Duston's supplied **The Long Tar (in progress)** manuscript, Chapters 3–6. The original manuscript is not redistributed inside this project or sent wholesale to a provider.

## Grounded in the manuscript

- Jade carries their infant Larcen and a dark sword through Nomand's Land after an ambush.
- They identify their pursuers as Crowley's men; this is a character inference.
- They observe the ecosystem, reason about the sand worm's scent, and find beauty in luminous insects amid danger.
- They reach Doc, entrust Larcen to him, and die. Doc later follows the trail and reckons with their persistence and care.

## Adapted for the app

Jade is the default editable engram. The companion's calm coaching voice, career exercises, they/them pronouns, and respect for user-led decisions are deliberate app adaptations. The manuscript uses she/her; the app follows the user's current reference to Jade as “their.” This is not a rewrite of the source.

The app does not equate perseverance with enduring harm or ignoring exhaustion. Rest, support and changing goals remain valid. It does not cast the user as Jade's child or claim that the fictional character is conscious, resurrected, or clinically therapeutic.

The draft contains a Liz/Jade name inconsistency in Chapter 5. Unresolved references to Don, the vault, the sword and pursuers remain unresolved. Do not add speculative details as canon. The manuscript's alternate history is fictional.

Only the compact editable biography is included in normal model prompts. The original prose stays in the author's supplied document. A future lore retrieval feature can be added separately; this release does not claim full-manuscript retrieval.
